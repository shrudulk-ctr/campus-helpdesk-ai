How the AI works
1. Understands your question — tokenises, expands synonyms, removes stopwords.
2. Retrieves — TF-IDF vectors + cosine similarity rank the supplied facts.
3. Generates — composes the answer from the top fact only, with its source and confidence.
4. Never invents — if a date, time or number isn't in the supplied information, it says so.
What the AI will not do It will not make up exam dates, library timings, fees, phone numbers or any fact not in the supplied information. That is a hard rule in the code, not a prompt suggestion.
